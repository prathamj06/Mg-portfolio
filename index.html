✦ ORIGIN & ETHOS

Peer mentorship rooted in first-principles discovery.

An informal collective where undergraduate freshers collaborate directly with founders on substantive hardware, software, and physical research.

We deconstruct concepts from axioms: deriving backpropagation before coding neural networks, synthesizing microstrip matching before printing circuit boards, and tracing continuous Lie algebras before modeling physical dynamics.

Comprising 30+ first-year undergraduate students, the collective connects theoretical curiosity with laboratory reality through weekly seminars, reading circles, and hands-on hardware sprints.

  • ✦

    Foundational Derivations

    Deconstructing mathematical axioms and physical limits before executing code.

  • ✦

    Laboratory Benchwork

    Demystifying physical systems with VNAs, SDR transceivers, logic probes, and FPGAs.

  • ✦

    Fresher-First Mentorship

    Every first-year researcher collaborates with a founder to design and ship an open prototype.

✦ COMMUNITY INITIATION

Join our technical collective.

Melerigamy is open to students driven by genuine curiosity in physical science, systems engineering, and mathematics. We require no prior credentials—only a willingness to read foundational texts, write code, build hardware, and experiment collaboratively.

  • ✦

    First-Year Friendly: Every new member is mentored by founders and integrated into hands-on reading groups and lab sprints.

  • ✦

    Open Hardware & Code: All project artifacts, schematics, and simulation models are archived openly for peer review.

  • ✦

    Weekly Technical Sessions: Participate in live teardowns, math seminars, and paper discussions every weekend.
